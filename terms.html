Terms and Refund Policy

These terms apply to AIM (AI in Marketing), a live online program by Adsmentor. They are written to match the program page. If anything here seems different from what you were told, email us before you pay. Effective 4 November 2026.

1. The program

AIM is a live, online, 12-week program: weeks 1 to 4 cover core skills, weeks 5 to 8 cover four specialisations, and weeks 9 to 12 are a one-month agency internship. It has nine modules, with AI built into every one. The syllabus and tracks are described on the home page.

2. Fee

  • AIM has two plans: ₹5,999 without tool access and ₹9,999 with tool access. These are the only two prices, and there are no hidden add-ons.
  • Both plans cover the training, all four specialisations, the agency internship and certificate, a portfolio website with domain and hosting, and a CV rewrite and mock interviews. The ₹9,999 plan also gives access to Claude, Semrush, Gemini Pro, Google AI Pro, Ahrefs, SpyFu, Ubersuggest and Canva. Ad spend for portfolio campaigns is not included.
  • You pay only after your onboarding call, once your questions are answered. We do not take payment on this website.

3. 7-day money-back guarantee

If AIM is not right for you, tell us within 7 days of your first live class and we will refund your program fee. To ask for a refund, email contact@adsmentor.in from the email address you enrolled with, within that 7-day window. No reason is required.

4. No job guarantee

We do not promise a job, a placement or a salary. AIM gives you real work, a month inside a partner agency, a portfolio and interview preparation. Examples of past student outcomes on this site come from the Adsmentor program behind AIM and are not a promise of your result.

5. Certificate

The internship certificate is issued by the partner agency after you complete the internship month.

6. Conduct and access

Classes and materials are for the enrolled learner only. Please do not share, resell or record live sessions without permission. We may remove access for misuse or abusive behaviour.

7. Changes to the program

Tools and platforms change quickly. We may update the syllabus, trainers, tools or schedule to keep the program current. If a change materially reduces what you were promised, contact us and we will work it out with you.
