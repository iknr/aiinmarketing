Questions, answered

What is vibe marketing?

Vibe marketing is a way of working where you direct AI tools to do much of the building, writing and analysis while you supply the taste, strategy and judgment. The name is borrowed from vibe coding. At AIM you learn to do it inside real tools and on company-style briefs, so speed does not cost you quality.

Who is AIM for: freelancers, agencies or jobs?

All three. Freelancers, people who want to work in or build agencies, and job seekers take the same program: the same nine modules with AI in every one, at the same fee.
