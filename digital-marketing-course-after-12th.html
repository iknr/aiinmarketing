Can you do digital marketing after 12th?

Yes, and 12th pass is enough to start. Digital marketing is a skill you can learn in months and use to get an entry-level job, an internship or your first freelance client, while you are still in college. You do not need a particular stream. You do not need to know coding. You do need to practise on real tools, because that is what employers test.

Digital marketing after 12th: commerce, science or arts

  • Commerce: Budgets, pricing and customer behaviour will feel familiar. Ads and media planning are a natural fit.
  • Science: You are used to data and logic, so analytics, tracking, SEO and automations tend to click quickly.
  • Arts and humanities: Writing, design and storytelling matter a lot in content, social media and branding.

The course starts from zero for everyone, so the stream you studied does not decide whether you can join. It only decides which module feels easiest at first.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do a digital marketing course after 12th?

Yes. You can start right after 12th, from any stream. AIM starts from building a website and goes step by step, so you do not need marketing experience.

Which stream is best for digital marketing after 12th: commerce, science or arts?

None is required. Commerce students find the business side familiar, science students often like the analytics, and arts students often like content and branding. The course teaches all of it from the start, so your stream does not decide whether you can join.

Do I need maths or coding for digital marketing?

No coding is needed. You use everyday numbers for budgets and reports, and you build your site with WordPress and AI tools instead of writing code from scratch.

Can I do the course along with college?

Yes. Classes are live online, Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 pm IST, so they fit around college hours. Recordings stay in the LMS if you miss a class.

Is a digital marketing degree better than a course?

A degree such as a BBA or B.Sc takes three years and costs much more, and it gives you a degree certificate. A short course teaches the working skills in weeks and gives you projects and an internship to show. Many students do both: a degree for the certificate and a course for the skills employers ask about in interviews.

What jobs can I get with digital marketing after 12th?

Common entry roles are SEO executive, social media executive, performance marketing or ads executive, content executive and e-commerce marketer. You can also freelance. Placement support is included: interview prep sessions, job application support and guidance.

How long does a digital marketing course after 12th take?

Courses run from about three months to three years depending on the type. AIM is 12 weeks: eight weeks of live classes and a 1-month agency internship.

Are online digital marketing courses valid?

Employers care about what you can show: a live website, campaign setups, an SEO audit and real client work. AIM gives you those, plus an internship certificate issued by the agency.

How much does it cost?

The AIM program costs ₹5,999 with limited tool access, or ₹9,999 with full tool access. A three-year degree costs several lakh rupees. See the fee comparison on this page.
