What a vibe marketing course should teach

Vibe marketing is the habit of telling AI what you want and steering the output, instead of doing each step by hand. It is only useful if you also know what good looks like, so a course has to teach both the tools and the marketing. Read the full explanation in what vibe marketing is and how to start.

How AIM teaches it

  • Build: a WordPress site and a landing page created by describing them, with WordPress MCP and AI coding tools.
  • Research and plan: Claude and Claude Code for audits, keyword clusters and content plans.
  • Run: ads on five platforms, DM and WhatsApp automations, chatbots and CRO tests.
  • Check: you review every output, because AI can be confidently wrong and the account is yours.

Two meanings of vibe marketing: which one this course is

You will see the term used two ways. Some brands use it for building a feeling around the brand, through style, culture and tone, instead of listing product features. In AI circles it means directing AI tools to build and run marketing work from plain-language instructions. This course teaches the second meaning, the hands-on one, and the brand side comes in through the Branding and Content specialisation.

Frequently asked questions

What is vibe marketing?

Vibe marketing means describing the marketing outcome you want in plain language and directing AI tools to build and run it, then checking and improving the result yourself. Our guide on what vibe marketing is explains it in full.

Do I need to code for a vibe marketing course?

No. Module 1 teaches vibe coding, where you describe a landing page to AI coding tools instead of writing it by hand, and you start from zero.

How much does the course cost?

It costs ₹5,999 with limited tool access, or ₹9,999 with full tool access. The training, all four specialisations and the 1-month agency internship are the same in both. Do I need marketing experience to join?

No. The program starts from building a website and goes step by step to AI-run workflows, so beginners can join.
