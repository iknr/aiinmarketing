1. The program

AIM is a live, online, 12-week program: weeks 1 to 4 cover core skills, weeks 5 to 8 cover four specialisations, and weeks 9 to 12 are a one-month agency internship. It has nine modules, with AI built into every one. The syllabus and tracks are described on the home page.

2. Fee

  • AIM has two plans: ₹5,999 with limited tool access and ₹9,999 with full tool access. These are the only two prices, and there are no hidden add-ons.
  • Both plans cover the training, all four specialisations, the agency internship and certificate, a portfolio website with domain and hosting, and a CV rewrite and mock interviews. The ₹9,999 plan also gives access to Claude, Semrush, Gemini Pro, Google AI Pro, Ahrefs, SpyFu, Ubersuggest and Canva. Live ad experience with a real ad budget is part of the ₹9,999 plan only.
