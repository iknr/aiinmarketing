What a digital marketing resume has to do

A recruiter will give your resume a few seconds. It has to show three things fast: what you can do, proof that you did it and a link they can click. For a fresher, that means projects, tools and a portfolio link, in that order of importance. A long career summary does not help when there is no career yet.

A clean one-page layout

  1. Header: name, phone, email, city, LinkedIn and portfolio link.
  2. Summary: two lines, your focus and your best proof.
  3. Skills and tools: grouped by area.
  4. Projects: three to four, each with a link.
  5. Internship or client work: if you have any.
  6. Education and certificates.

Use one simple font, plain headings and no photo, tables or graphics. Many hiring tools read plain text better than designed layouts.

Writing the summary

Two lines, in plain words. Example: Digital marketing fresher focused on SEO and content, with a live portfolio site, an SEO audit and a 3-month roadmap for a real brand. Completed a 1-month agency internship. Replace each part with what is true for you.

Project and internship bullets that read well

Start with what you did, name the tool, then say why or what came of it. Do not invent numbers.

Skills and tools: how to list them

  • Search: keyword research, on-page SEO, Search Console, Semrush
  • Content and social: content calendars, Canva, scheduling, DM automation
  • Paid ads: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager
  • Analytics: GA4, Tag Manager basics, simple reports
  • Web and AI: WordPress, Claude and other AI tools, landing pages

Keep only what you can talk about in an interview.

Mistakes that cost interviews

  • Two or more pages with little proof.
  • A skills bar or star ratings. They tell the reader nothing.
  • Invented results or inflated job titles.
  • Projects with no links.
  • One resume for every job. Change the summary and the order of skills for each role.

Frequently asked questions

What should a digital marketing resume for a fresher include?

A short summary, a skills and tools section, projects with links, any internship or client work, education and a portfolio link. Put projects above education if you have no job history.

How long should a fresher resume be?

One page. A recruiter scans it in seconds, so every line has to earn its place.

What if I have no experience?

Use projects. A live site, an SEO audit, a content calendar and campaign setups show what you can do. Say they are practice projects and link to each.

Should I put numbers on my resume?

Only real ones. If you ran a real account, give the real result. If a project was practice, describe what you built and decided instead of inventing a result.

Which skills should I list?

Group them: SEO, content and social, paid ads, analytics, web and AI tools. List only tools you can use in an interview, and name them: for example WordPress, Search Console, GA4, Meta Ads Manager, Canva and Claude.

Do I need a portfolio link on the resume?

Yes. It is the most useful line on the page. Put it at the top beside your contact details.

Should I mention AI tools?

Yes, with a clear line on what you used them for and what you checked yourself. Employers want people who can direct AI tools, not just name them.
