What a good digital marketing project looks like

A good project ends in something a person can open or check. A live page. A plan with reasons. A campaign setup with a test plan. A report with screenshots and no thinking behind it does not convince anyone. If you are a student or a fresher, three to five strong projects usually matter more than a long list.

How to run each project

  1. Write a one-paragraph brief: who the business is, what it sells, the goal.
  2. Research before you build: competitors, audience, search terms.
  3. Build the thing: the page, plan or setup, with AI tools where they save time.
  4. Explain your choices: why this audience, this budget split, this headline.
  5. Publish it: one page per project on your site, linked from LinkedIn.

Frequently asked questions

What are good digital marketing projects for students?

Pick projects that end in something a person can open or check: a live website, an SEO audit with a roadmap, a 30-day content calendar, a campaign setup with a test plan and a media plan for a launch. Reports with only screenshots are weak proof.

Can I do digital marketing projects with no clients?

Yes. Use a real local business, a brand you already know or your own site. Say it is a practice brief. Employers care more about how you think and what you built than about whether money changed hands.

How many projects do I need in my portfolio?

Three to five solid ones beat fifteen thin ones. Cover a site, search, content and ads at least once each.

Is there a digital marketing projects PDF I can download?

Yes. The free Digital Marketing Portfolio Kit is a 7-page PDF that shows how to present your projects. Get it from the portfolio guide linked on this page.

What projects do you build in the AIM course?

A live portfolio website and landing page, an SEO audit with a 3-month roadmap, a 30-day content calendar with one working DM or WhatsApp automation, campaign setups on five ad platforms with a test plan, and a full media plan for a brand launch. Specialisations and the agency internship add more.

Do I need to spend money on ads for a project?

No. A campaign setup with targeting, creative, budget logic and a test plan shows the skill without spend. On the ₹9,999 plan you also get live ad experience on an agency or brand account with a real ad budget.

Can I use AI tools in my projects?

Yes, and you should. Say clearly what AI did and what you decided. Employers want to see that you can direct the tools, check the output and fix it.

Where do I show my projects?

On your own portfolio website, with one page per project, plus your LinkedIn featured section and your CV.
