How the online course works

Classes run live, Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 pm IST. You work inside the platforms yourself, not on a trainer’s screen. Each week has one assignment that goes into your portfolio, and weekends are for one-on-one doubt clearing, project reviews and interview prep.

What AIM does that most digital marketing courses do not

  • AI in every module, not one chapter. From week 1 you work with Claude, WordPress MCP, MCPs for ad accounts, CRO automations and chatbots.
  • You work inside the real platforms. You log in and do the work yourself instead of watching a trainer’s screen.
  • A real agency month. Weeks 9 to 12 are client tasks at a partner agency with a weekly mentor check-in, and the certificate comes from the agency.
  • Four specialisations, all included. AI Generalist, Marktech, Branding and Content, and Performance and Growth, with no add-on fee.
  • Two openly listed fees, one training. ₹5,999 with limited tool access or ₹9,999 with full tool access. Only tool access and live ad experience differ.

The internship is online too

Weeks 9 to 12 are a 1-month internship at a partner agency on real client tasks, with a mentor check-in every week. The certificate comes from the agency, so it shows real work and not only that you finished classes.

Frequently asked questions

Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

They are live, Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 pm IST. Weekends are used for one-on-one doubt clearing, project reviews and interview prep.

Is an online digital marketing course as good as a classroom one?

It can be, if the classes are live and you do real work. AIM is live, with weekly assignments for your portfolio, one-on-one doubt clearing on weekends and a 1-month online agency internship.

Can I do it while working or studying?

Yes. The evening schedule was set so learners can join alongside a job or college.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop and a stable internet connection. The portfolio website, domain and hosting are included, and the ₹9,999 plan adds tool access.

Is there a refund if it is not right for me?

Yes. There is a 7-day money-back guarantee. Tell us within 7 days of your first live class, with at least 70% attendance in those days, and the program fee is refunded. You pay only after an onboarding call, once your questions are answered.

Do you offer placement support?

Yes. Placement support is included: interview prep sessions, job application support and guidance, along with real work, an agency internship and a CV rewrite. Recent students have been placed at ₹5 to 15 LPA, with public profiles you can check.
