What a vibe marketing course should teach

Vibe marketing is the habit of telling AI what you want and steering the output, instead of doing each step by hand. It is only useful if you also know what good looks like, so a course has to teach both the tools and the marketing. Read the full explanation in what vibe marketing is and how to start.

How AIM teaches it

  • Build: a WordPress site and a landing page created by describing them, with WordPress MCP and AI coding tools.
  • Research and plan: Claude and Claude Code for audits, keyword clusters and content plans.
  • Run: ads on five platforms, DM and WhatsApp automations, chatbots and CRO tests.
  • Check: you review every output, because AI can be confidently wrong and the account is yours.

The 12-week path

  • Also included: a live portfolio site on your own domain and hosting, a CV rewrite and 3 mock interviews.
