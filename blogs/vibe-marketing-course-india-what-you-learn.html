A vibe marketing course should teach you to tell AI tools what you want, steer what comes back and check it before a client sees it. Some courses sell the name and teach a list of prompts. Here is what to look for before you pay.

Key takeaways
  • Vibe marketing means describing the result you want, letting AI build it, then checking the work yourself.
  • A good course teaches the marketing and the tools together. Tools alone go out of date quickly.
  • You do not need to code. You do need to judge whether the output is right.
  • Ask for real projects, tool access and an internship. Ask for the full price in writing.

People now use "vibe marketing" for a way of working: you describe a landing page, an ad set or a content plan in plain language, an AI tool produces a draft, and you steer it until it is right. If you want the plain-language basics first, read what vibe marketing is. This guide is for the next question: what should a course actually teach?

What a vibe marketing course is

It is a marketing course where AI tools do much of the typing, building and first drafting, and you learn to direct them. The name does not mean you stop thinking. I would say it means you spend less time on the keyboard and more time deciding what good looks like. A course that skips that second part is only teaching button clicks.

Five skills it should teach

  • Describing the outcome clearly. Vague requests give vague work. You should practise writing briefs that name the audience, the offer, the format and the limits. Our RTCROS prompt guide shows one way to do it.
  • Building without hand coding. A website and a landing page made by describing them to AI tools, with WordPress MCP and AI coding tools, from a standing start.
  • Research and planning. Audits, keyword clusters, competitor checks and content plans, run with Claude and an SEO tool.
  • Running campaigns. Ads on the main platforms, DM and WhatsApp automations, chatbots and simple conversion tests.
  • Checking the result. AI can be confidently wrong, and the ad account or the website belongs to the client. You should leave knowing how to review every output, and what to do when it fails.

Tools you should work in

Ask which tools you will log into yourself. For vibe marketing that usually means an AI assistant such as Claude, an AI coding tool, WordPress, an SEO tool such as Semrush or Ahrefs, a design tool such as Canva, and the ad platforms. Tools change every few months, so a good course teaches the habit of picking and testing tools, not only a fixed list. Perhaps the simplest test is this: after the course, could you pick up a tool that did not exist today and use it on a real brief?

Who it suits, and who should wait

  • It suits students, career switchers, freelancers and small business owners who want to do marketing work faster with AI.
  • It suits working marketers who want proof that they can run AI-assisted work, not only talk about it.
  • Wait if you want a certificate and no practice. Vibe marketing rewards people who build things.
  • Wait if you will not review the output. Skipping the check is where most of the expensive mistakes come from.

Questions to ask before you pay

  • Is AI in every module, or in one chapter at the end?
  • Will I use the tools myself from the first weeks?
  • What will I have built when the course ends, and can I show it?
  • Who are the trainers, and can I check their LinkedIn profiles?
  • What is the total fee, with tools, GST and certificate? Is it in writing?
  • What is the refund rule?

Frequently asked questions

What is a vibe marketing course?

A vibe marketing course teaches you to describe marketing outcomes in plain language, direct AI tools to build and run them, and check the results yourself. It covers both the marketing and the tools.

Do I need to code to learn vibe marketing?

No. You describe what you want, such as a landing page, and AI coding tools build it. You do need to review the result, so you learn the basics of what a good page looks like.

How long does it take to learn vibe marketing?

The AIM program runs 12 weeks, including a 1-month agency internship. Basic comfort with the tools usually comes in the first few weeks, and judgement comes with practice on real briefs.

How much does a vibe marketing course cost at AIM?

It costs Rs 5,999 without tool access, or Rs 9,999 with tool access. The training and internship are the same in both plans. There is a 7-day money-back guarantee.
