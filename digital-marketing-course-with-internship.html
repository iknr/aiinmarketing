What the internship is

The internship is weeks 9 to 12 of the 12-week program. You spend a month at a partner agency on real client tasks, with a mentor check-in every week. At the end you get a certificate issued by the agency, so it shows real work and not only that you finished classes.

What you do before it starts

Weeks 1 to 8 are live classes, Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 pm IST. Weeks 1 to 4 cover the core skills with AI in every module. Weeks 5 to 8 are four specialisations: AI Generalist, Marktech, Branding and Content, and Performance and Growth. By the time the internship starts you have a live portfolio site and a project from each module.

What you take away

  • A month of real client work to show in interviews or to freelance clients.
  • An agency certificate, not only a course certificate.
  • A live portfolio site on your own domain and hosting.
  • A CV rewrite and 3 mock interviews.

Placement support is part of the program: interview prep sessions, job application support and guidance.

Course with internship or course without: what to check

Many courses say internship and mean a project inside the class. Ask four things before you pay: Is it at a real agency? Is it on client work? Does someone review it each week? Does the certificate come from the agency? If any answer is no, it is a class project.

Internship near me: why location does not matter here

Many students search for a digital marketing internship near them and find only a few agencies, often unpaid and without training. The AIM internship is online, so your city does not limit you. If you are looking for more routes, read how to get a digital marketing internship in India.

Is it paid?

No. It is a training internship, which is why the fee covers the training and the internship together. You get real client work, a mentor and a certificate from the agency instead of a stipend. Be careful with any course that promises a paid internship or a guaranteed job.

How to make the most of it

  1. Finish your portfolio site before week 9, so you have a place to publish the work.
  2. Keep a short log of tasks and what you decided.
  3. Ask your mentor for feedback each week and use it.
  4. Write up your best task as a one-page case, without client secrets.

Frequently asked questions

Is the internship really included in the fee?

Yes. The 1-month agency internship is weeks 9 to 12 of the program and is in both plans, ₹5,999 and ₹9,999. There is no separate internship fee.

Is the internship paid?

No. It is a training internship. You get real client tasks, a weekly mentor check-in and an agency certificate, not a stipend.

Where is the internship done?

Online, like the rest of the program. You do not need to move to a city or travel to an office.

What work do I do in the internship?

Real client tasks at a partner agency, in the areas you trained in, such as SEO, content, ads and automation. A mentor checks in every week and the work goes into your portfolio.

Do you help with jobs after the internship?

Yes. Placement support covers interview prep sessions, job application support and guidance, along with a CV rewrite, 3 mock interviews, a portfolio site and real work to show from the internship.

Is there a digital marketing internship near me?

You do not need one near you. The AIM internship is online, so you can do it from any city in India while the agency works with you remotely. That is useful if there is no good agency near you, or you cannot travel.

Can I do the internship while in college?

Yes. Classes are in the evening, 9 to 11 pm IST, so they fit around college hours. Plan your internship month around your timetable and talk to your mentor about it.

Do I get an internship certificate?

Yes. The certificate is issued by the agency where you worked, along with the course completion.

Which agency will I be placed with?

The agency is matched to your projects and your strongest area, so the work fits what you trained in.

What is the difference between a course project and an agency internship?

A course project is an exercise in a class. An agency internship is real client work, reviewed by a mentor every week, with a certificate from the agency. Ask any course which one it means.

How much does the course cost?

It costs ₹5,999 with limited tool access, or ₹9,999 with full tool access. The training, all four specialisations and the 1-month agency internship are the same in both. The ₹9,999 plan adds access to Claude, Semrush, Gemini Pro, Google AI Pro, Ahrefs, SpyFu, Ubersuggest and Canva. Only this plan includes live ad experience on an agency or brand account with a real ad budget. The ₹5,999 plan has no ad budget, and there is no other add-on.

Do I need marketing experience to join?

No. The program starts from building a website and goes step by step to AI-run workflows, so beginners can join.

Is there a refund if it is not right for me?

Yes. There is a 7-day money-back guarantee. Tell us within 7 days of your first live class, with at least 70% attendance in those days, and the program fee is refunded. You pay only after an onboarding call, once your questions are answered.
