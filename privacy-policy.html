Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information AIM (AI in Marketing), a program by Adsmentor, collects through aiinmarketing.in, why we collect it and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose. Effective date: 4 November 2026.

1. Who we are

AIM (AI in Marketing) is an AI-first digital marketing training program run by Adsmentor (‘we’, ‘us’). You can reach us at contact@adsmentor.in or +91 83406 47293 for anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, WhatsApp or mobile number and email address when you apply, ask a question or download a free guide, plus where you are in your career if the form asks for it.
  • Messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Technical and usage information: pages visited, approximate location (city level), device and browser type and how you move around the site, collected through cookies and similar tools described below. When you submit a form we also record your IP address and approximate location (country, region and city) so we can keep our forms secure and prevent spam and abuse.

We do not collect payment details on this website. The program fee is only requested after your onboarding call, through the payment method we share with you at that time.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and book your onboarding call.
  • To send the free guide you asked for.
  • To run and improve the program and this website.
  • To measure which pages and campaigns work.
  • To use analytics and marketing tools to understand how people use the site and to improve our marketing and the way we communicate with you.
  • To keep our forms and the site secure and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We contact you only about what you asked for or about the program. You can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use tracking and analytics tools such as Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, and similar marketing and measurement tools, to capture details such as the pages you visit, how you move around a page, your device and browser, your approximate location and, in Clarity’s case, anonymised heatmaps and session recordings. Form fields that hold personal details are masked and are not recorded. We use this to improve the site, our marketing and the communication experience. These tools use cookies and similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use browser or extension settings to opt out of Google Analytics and Clarity; the site keeps working without them.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Adsmentor, the company behind AIM;
  • service providers that help us run the program and website, such as our hosting provider (Vercel), analytics providers (Google, Microsoft) and our messaging and email tools;
  • our customer relationship management (CRM) tool, Heffl, our service provider, where the details you give us in forms are stored; and
  • authorities where the law requires it.

These providers may process data in countries other than India under their own privacy terms.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to run the program, then delete or anonymise them. If you ask us to delete your details sooner, we will, unless the law requires us to keep something.

7. Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

  • ask to see the information we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • have it erased;
  • withdraw consent; or
  • raise a complaint.

Email contact@adsmentor.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Our program is meant for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please apply with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian. If we learn we hold a child’s information without that consent, we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information. No online service can promise perfect security, so please share only what the forms ask for.

10. Links to other sites

This site links to other websites such as Amazon, LinkedIn and Adsmentor. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: contact@adsmentor.in, +91 83406 47293.
