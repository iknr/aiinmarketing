About

Kailas Nath R is a digital marketing and agentic AI consultant at Big Stone Agency and a trainer in the AIM program. On the AIM home page he is listed with 6+ years of experience, delivering and auditing GTM and CRM automation builds across live client accounts. He co-wrote The Ad Account Nobody Shows You with Kautilya Roshan, a hands-on guide to paid social. He writes the guides on this blog together with Kautilya, and the aim is always the same: plain steps you can try on your own work this week.
