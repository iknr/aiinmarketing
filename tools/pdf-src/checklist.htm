The 5-question course checklist

Ask any digital marketing institute these before you pay.

Fees in India range from under ₹20,000 to over ₹3 lakh for similar-sounding syllabi. These five questions show what you are really paying for.

1. Is AI taught in every module, or in one chapter at the end?

☐ Ask to see the module list and where AI tools are used.
☐ Ask what you will actually build with AI, not what you will hear about.

2. Will I practise inside the real tools?

☐ Ask which tools you get access to, for how long, and how many learners share a login.
☐ Be careful with "tool access" that means one login for 10 to 20 people.

3. Will I leave with proof of work?

☐ Ask for a live portfolio, real client work and a certificate from a working agency.
☐ Ask who owns the accounts and the work in your portfolio.

4. Who exactly teaches, and are they working today?

☐ Ask for LinkedIn profiles and check their current roles.

5. What is the full price?

☐ Ask if the fee includes GST, tools, internship and certification, and when you pay.
☐ Ask what happens if it is not a fit after the first session.
