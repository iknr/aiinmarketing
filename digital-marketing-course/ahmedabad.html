How the live online course works for learners in Ahmedabad

There is no classroom in Ahmedabad to travel to. You log in for two hours each weekday evening (9 to 11 pm IST), and weekends are for doubt clearing, project reviews and interview prep. Routine marketing work is moving to AI tools. What stays with people is directing those tools and checking the output. So AI is part of every module, and you work inside the real platforms from the first week.
