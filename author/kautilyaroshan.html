About

Kautilya Roshan is a generative AI engineer, LinkedIn Top Voice, consultant and trainer who teaches advertising in the AIM program. On the AIM home page he is introduced as an advertising consultant and former programmatic consultant for SIDA Digital, with 8+ years of experience. The site notes that he has consulted for enterprise and retail brands and trained 9,000+ marketers. He co-wrote The Ad Account Nobody Shows You with Kailas Nath R, and the two of them write the guides on this blog.
