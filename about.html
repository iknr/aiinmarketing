About us

Real marketing learning should not be a luxury

AIM exists for one reason: to make real marketing learning, with AI built in, affordable and accessible for everyone who is willing to work for it.

Who we are

AIM (AI in Marketing) is an AI-first digital marketing program by Adsmentor. We are practising marketers and trainers who run ad accounts, build websites and automate funnels for a living. We teach the way the work is done today, with AI in every step, inside the real platforms, so what you learn is what you will use.

Our four trainers have public profiles you can check, with ₹9 Cr+ in combined media spend managed. You finish with a 1-month internship at a partner agency, so you walk into interviews with a work history, not only a certificate.

Why we exist

Think of the graduate whose family has stretched every rupee for a degree, the professional who wants a better role without quitting a job, the small business owner who cannot afford an agency yet. These are exactly the people who benefit most from modern marketing skills, and too often they are priced out of learning them.

Marketing changed fast. AI is now how the work gets done, yet many courses still add one AI chapter at the end and charge accordingly. We think that is backwards. Learning that can change someone's career should not depend on how much money they have today.

What you get from learning with us

  • Confidence at work. AI runs through every module, so you finish tasks faster and with better results than people still learning it as theory.
  • Skills that stick. You work inside the platforms yourself, on briefs shaped like real company needs, instead of watching a trainer's screen.
  • Proof you can show. A live portfolio and an agency internship certificate say more in an interview than a completion badge.
  • Peace of mind on cost. One clear fee, no hidden add-ons, paid after your questions are answered.

We do not promise a job. We give you real work, an agency month and interview prep. Recent students have been placed at ₹5 to 15 LPA, with public profiles you can verify.
