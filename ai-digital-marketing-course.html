Digital marketing with AI: AI in every module, not one chapter

Many courses add one AI chapter near the end. Here AI runs through all nine modules, so you never learn a habit you will have to unlearn. In week 1 you are already working inside the tools.

  • WordPress MCP: connect Claude to a live WordPress site and direct it to build pages and fix issues. You stay the editor.
  • Claude and Claude Code: audits, reports and content batches in the command line that used to take whole days.
  • MCPs for ads: ask your ad accounts what is wasting spend, and review the fixes before anything goes live.
  • CRO automations: find where visitors drop off and keep tests running.
  • Chatbots: website and WhatsApp bots that answer, qualify and book leads.
  • Agents: research competitors and draft reports for your review.

Frequently asked questions

What makes this an AI digital marketing course and not a normal one?

AI is part of all nine modules and all four specialisations, not one chapter. You work with Claude, Claude Code and the command line, WordPress MCP, MCPs for ads, CRO automations and chatbots, on real-style briefs.

Which AI tools do I get access to?

The ₹9,999 plan includes tool access: Claude, Semrush, Gemini Pro, Google AI Pro, Ahrefs, SpyFu, Ubersuggest and Canva. The ₹5,999 plan has no tool access, and the training is the same. Many tools in the course also have free plans or trials.

What is a digital marketing with AI course?

It teaches the usual digital marketing skills, which are SEO, ads, social media, content and analytics, with AI tools used inside each one. At AIM the tools include Claude, WordPress MCP and MCPs for ad accounts, used on real-style briefs.

Is an AI digital marketing course worth it in 2026?

It is worth it if AI is in every module, you do real work with it and you leave with proof. Check three things before paying: AI inside every module, an internship or real client work, and a fee listed openly. AIM lists both fees and has a 7-day money-back guarantee.

How long is the AI digital marketing course?

Twelve weeks. Weeks 1 to 4 are core skills, weeks 5 to 8 are four specialisations, and weeks 9 to 12 are a 1-month agency internship. Live classes run Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 pm IST.

Will AI replace marketers?

AI now does most routine marketing tasks, so the routine part of the job is shrinking. People who know how to direct AI, check its output and own the result are the ones companies keep. That is what the course trains.

Do I need marketing experience to join?

No. The program starts from building a website and goes step by step to AI-run workflows, so beginners can join.

Is there a refund if it is not right for me?

Yes. There is a 7-day money-back guarantee. Tell us within 7 days of your first live class, with at least 70% attendance in those days, and the program fee is refunded. You pay only after an onboarding call, once your questions are answered.
